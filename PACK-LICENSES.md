# Pack licences

Every pack the Vault downloads, its licence, and the credit it asks for. Checked October 2026. This is a research summary, not legal advice.

- **Share freely:** public domain or an open licence. Credit it, and keep share-alike packs under the same licence.
- **Free only:** a non-commercial licence. Fine to give away; never sell, rent, charge for or add ads.
- **Ask first:** fine for your own reading. Get the owner's written permission before handing out copies.
- **Unclear:** no licence found. Treat it like Ask first until it's checked.

The Get files only download Share freely and Free only packs. The others can be picked in Choose Packs.bat, for your own use.
