What it is, and what it isn't

A huge shelf of knowledge, not a magician

The Vault puts a vast amount of information within reach of people who otherwise wouldn't have it. That's all it does, and it's a lot. But it's a library: it helps the people who can use it, alongside everything else they already rely on.

It can

  • Put millions of pages within reach with no connection at all.
  • Help someone look something up, learn a skill or prepare ahead.
  • Give a teacher, health worker, farmer or builder a reference to work from.

It can't

  • Examine a patient, test your water or check a wiring job.
  • Know your situation, your climate or your local rules.
  • Keep itself current. Each pack is a snapshot, and some are years old.
  • Stand in for trained people, local knowledge or emergency help.

Use it next to the people around you. When something is urgent or dangerous, reach someone trained, in person, if you can. The Vault is there to help wherever it can.

How it works

Three steps, then no internet needed

  1. Download this project

    Unzip it onto a big drive, with Vault Search.exe beside it. The library itself comes next, straight from its official sources.

  2. Double-click a Get file

    Pick a subject, like Get - Health and Medicine.bat. It asks where to save and resumes if the internet drops.

  3. Open Vault Search

    Double-click Vault Search.exe. One search box opens in your browser. Type what you want to know.

What to download

One file per subject

Each Get file downloads only packs you're allowed to share. Bars are to scale against everything shareable together.

Choose Packs.bat picks packs one by one. Check for Updates.bat finds newer versions. Make a Vault Drive.bat copies the library and Vault Search onto a USB drive or SD card to hand to someone: they plug it in and double-click.

Giving copies away

Five rules that keep it legal

  • Always freeNever sell, rent or charge for the drive, the box or access, and no ads. That keeps every non-commercial pack legal.
  • Keep the credits with itPut PACK-LICENSES.md on every copy. It carries the credit each licence asks for.
  • Keep packs wholeShare them unchanged. A few licences forbid edits.
  • No logos or endorsementThis project isn't affiliated with Wikipedia, Kiwix, World Possible or any publisher.
  • Medical packs are referenceEach pack shows its version date. Check with a health worker.

The library belongs to its creators: thousands of Wikipedia, Stack Exchange and OpenStreetMap contributors, and every publisher in PACK-LICENSES.md. Thanks to Kiwix and World Possible, who package and serve these libraries for free.
